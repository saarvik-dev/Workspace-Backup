## Why start / middle don’t work ❌

### ❌ Imagining the start

At the start:

- Nothing is solved yet
- You don’t know what future calls will do
- Too many unknowns → mental freeze
### ❌ Imagining the middle

The middle is:

- Multiple stack frames active
- Pointers half-updated
- Worst possible place to think
This is why recursion feels “mystical”.


---

## Why imagining the END works ✅

At the end:

- The base case is done
- The smallest problem is solved
- Now recursion **returns with results**
That’s where logic is clean.
